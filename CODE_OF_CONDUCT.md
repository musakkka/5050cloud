# Code of Conduct

5050cloud is built by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We want taking part to be a good experience for everyone.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give and take criticism about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and in any space that represents the project.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainers through GitHub: use **Report content** on the comment or issue, or contact a maintainer listed on the repository directly. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

Maintainers review every report and may edit or remove content, warn, or temporarily or permanently ban someone from the project, depending on what happened. Maintainers who do not follow this code may be removed from the project.
